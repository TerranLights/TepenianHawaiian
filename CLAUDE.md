# Standing rules for this repo

## What this repo is
A dedicated repo for researching, synthesizing and establishing **Modern Hawaiian** (Tepenian Hawaiian), a fictional language in the Inner Tepenia universe. The Inner Tepenia GDD repo is the authority on canon (`/home/kuroskalacs/Documents/Doll-Fi/media/games/Inner Tepenia/InnerTepeniaGDD/`; its note on this language is `Worldspace/Modern_Hawaiian_Language.md`). This repo is the working shop for the language itself.

## Rules
1. **Derive, do not invent.** Every word, sound, rule and idiom is derived from the contributing languages and from real contact-language behavior, through a recorded chain (see `synthesis_method/`). A form with no recorded derivation is not allowed into `language/`.
2. **Provenance on everything.** Each lexical entry and each grammar rule records its sources, the stage at which it entered the language, and its status. Sources are real reference works; record page or section. Selective quotes and paraphrase only, never bulk reproduction (copyright).
3. **Status discipline.** Everything is `proposed` until the developer accepts it, and the acceptance is logged in `DECISIONS.md`. Revise through a revision history, never by silent overwrite.
4. **Real research, done fully.** Research is the input data layer. A recalled linguistic fact is a guess; a wrong one is worse than a gap. Query from more than one angle, log dead ends, and verify against primary or scholarly sources. There is no credit for finishing quickly.
5. **The language is its own thing.** It is not English with word swaps, not Japanese with word swaps, and not a mixture by committee. Contact, nativization, regularization and 500 years of change produce something with its own logic.
6. **No national stereotypes.** Contributing languages are inputs; they say nothing about the character of people who speak the result.
7. **American English** for all prose in this repo.
8. **Nothing flows back into the GDD without an explicit decision.** The GDD note is updated only when the developer says so.
9. **Operating hours:** none for now (developer, 2026-10-13). Work here may run at any hour, including autonomous and unattended batches, because the Inner Tepenia ULM work does not resume until November (when the Max20x plan is paid for). The sibling repo `slang-synthesis` limits autonomous work to 3:00 pm to 4:59 am; that does not apply here. **Revisit when the ULM resumes.**
10. **Fictional-language terms in the GDD:** the GDD calls Dome Fuji "The Temple" until this language has produced its proper name (a Modern Hawaiian vocabulary term). Do not coin that name early.

## Era vocabulary (canon, 2026-10-13)
Each era calls its own language "Modern Hawaiian." From the Mars era, the Falkland-era language is "Middle Hawaiian," and today's real Hawaiian is "Ancient Hawaiian." In the Falkland/Federation era today's Hawaiian is "Old" or "Medieval" Hawaiian. Always say which vantage point a name is from.
