# Tepenian Hawaiian
a centralized repo for collecting, analyzing, and re-synthesizing data for Tepenian Hawaiian from across languages (notably English, Japanese, and others)

## Project Purpose

This repo researches, synthesizes and establishes **Modern Hawaiian**, the language of the Tepenian universe: a complete language that takes shape over the ~500 years between the Great War (2083) and the Falkland Treaty (2564), starting as a pidgin of English, Japanese and Tagalog in Hawaii, and becoming the international robot language and then the national language of the Tepenian Federation. It is synthesized as its own complete language, not studied for slang (that is the sibling repo, `slang-synthesis`).

The intended outputs are a series of eBooks: a grammar reference, a full dictionary, and a couple of history books.

**Start at [`00_START_HERE.md`](00_START_HERE.md)** for the reading order and the map of the repo. Standing rules are in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), established facts in [`CANON.md`](CANON.md), the phases in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).
