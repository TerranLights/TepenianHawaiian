# Provenance and status rules

Every lexical entry, grammar rule and history claim carries:
- **Status:** `proposed` / `accepted` / `superseded`.
- **Derivation chain:** source language(s) and form(s), stage of entry, each sound or meaning change with its approximate period.
- **Basis:** canon reference (`CANON.md`), decision (`DECISIONS.md`), or research source.
- **Revision history:** a dated table; never silent overwrite.
- **Confidence:** `high` (directly attested in a source), `medium` (derived by a stated rule), `low` (analogy only).

Reference works are paraphrased and quoted selectively, never reproduced in bulk.
